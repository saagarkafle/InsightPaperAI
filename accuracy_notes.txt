Current metrics
- QA Exact Match: 0.0000
- QA F1: 0.0652
- Avg LLM latency: 35.08s
- Offline abstract-based baseline: EM 0.0000, F1 0.1230, key-point recall 0.0000
- Retrieval labels are still missing, so recall@k and MRR are not being computed yet.

What was used to get these results
- Embeddings: sentence-transformers all-MiniLM-L6-v2
- Vector search: Pinecone plus local cosine retrieval
- Chunking: chunk_size=500, overlap=100
- LLM: Groq llama-3.1-8b-instant through OpenAI-style client
- Prompting: answer_question system prompt that restricts answers to retrieved context
- Token control: max_tokens reduced to 256
- Rate-limit control: 1 second sleep between LLM calls
- Evaluation scripts: scripts/eval_rag.py, scripts/eval_llm_abstract_gold.py, scripts/auto_eval_keypoints.py
- Gold answers: added to data/eval.jsonl from paper abstracts

Notes
- The current QA scores are low because the gold answers are short abstract-based references and the model answers have little exact overlap.
- Latency is high mainly because of remote LLM calls.
- To improve accuracy next, add retrieval labels to data/eval.jsonl, tighten chunk selection, and test smaller max_tokens or a different model.
